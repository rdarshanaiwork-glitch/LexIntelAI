# Landmark Judicial Precedent: Vishaka & Ors. v. State of Rajasthan & Anr.

* **Citation**: AIR 1997 SC 3011 / (1997) 6 SCC 241
* **Court**: Supreme Court / Appellate Division
* **Date**: 1997-08-13
* **Source Type**: precedent
* **Jurisdiction**: Common Law / Constitutional Safety
* **Legal Domain**: Workplace Safety & Gender Protection

## Ratio Decidendi & Core Legal Principles

1. **Employer Strict Liability for Safe Working Environment**:
   The Court held that every woman has a fundamental right to a safe working environment free from hostile harassment, intimidation, and sexual misconduct. Employer liability is strictly engaged where the management fails to implement preventive protocols, complaint mechanisms, and secure transport guarantees.

2. **Prohibition of Retaliatory Adverse Action**:
   The ruling established that an employee who reports workplace harassment or safety violations must not be victimized, transferred, or terminated under pretextual grounds. Where termination closely succeeds an internal grievance, the burden of proof shifts entirely to the employer to establish legitimate, non-retaliatory necessity by clear and convincing evidence.

3. **Mandatory Compliance with Complaint Redressal Machinery**:
   Actions taken against a complainant without prior, fair evaluation by a properly constituted Complaints Committee constitute a denial of due process and statutory natural justice.
