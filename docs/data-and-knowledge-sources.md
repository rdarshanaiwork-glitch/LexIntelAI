# LexIntel AI: Data & Knowledge Sources

## Ethical and Public Data Standards

LexIntel AI is built strictly on **public-domain statutes, restatements, landmark case law, and synthetic demonstration records**:

- **Model Restatements & Codes**:
  - Restatement (Second) of Contracts §§ 241, 356
  - Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) § 2-718
  - Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA), 18 U.S.C. § 1836
  - Uniform Trade Secrets Act (UTSA)
  - Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX) § 806, 18 U.S.C. § 1514A
  - Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA), 18 U.S.C. § 1030

- **Landmark Judicial Precedents**:
  - *Lawson v. FMR LLC*, 571 U.S. 429 (2014) (Whistleblower protection for contractors)
  - *Van Buren v. United States*, 593 U.S. 374 (2021) (CFAA gates-up technological access model)
  - *Hadley v. Baxendale*, 9 Exch. 341 (1854) (Foreseeability of consequential damages)
  - *Campbell v. Acuff-Rose Music, Inc.*, 510 U.S. 569 (1994) (Copyright fair use and transformative parody)
  - *Digital Realty Trust, Inc. v. Somers*, 583 U.S. 149 (2018) (Dodd-Frank reporting definitions)

> **Important**: LexIntel AI does NOT scrape, crawl, or access proprietary legal databases (e.g., Westlaw, LexisNexis, Bloomberg Law). All materials are sourced from open legal repositories and official government publishers.

## 3 Built-In Demonstration Matters

1. **Whistleblower Retaliation** (*Vance v. Sterling BioPharm International LLC*)
   - *Jurisdiction*: Federal (1st Cir. / D. Mass.)
   - *Statute*: 18 U.S.C. § 1514A (Sarbanes-Oxley Act § 806)
   - *Precedent*: *Lawson v. FMR LLC*, 571 U.S. 429 (2014)

2. **Cybersecurity & Data Exfiltration** (*OmniTech Cloud Services Inc. v. Marcus & NexaCore Cyber Ltd.*)
   - *Jurisdiction*: Federal (9th Cir. / N.D. Cal.)
   - *Statute*: 18 U.S.C. § 1030 (Computer Fraud and Abuse Act)
   - *Precedent*: *Van Buren v. United States*, 593 U.S. 374 (2021)

3. **Commercial Delay & Penalty Clauses** (*Apex Logistics Solutions LLC v. Horizon Retail Enterprise Inc.*)
   - *Jurisdiction*: Commercial / Common Law (2d Cir. / S.D.N.Y.)
   - *Statute*: Restatement (Second) of Contracts § 356; UCC § 2-718
   - *Precedent*: *Hadley v. Baxendale*, 9 Exch. 341 (1854)
