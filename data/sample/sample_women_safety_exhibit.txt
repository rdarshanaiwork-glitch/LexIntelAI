1. COMPLAINANT DETAILS:
   - Name: Priya Sharma
   - Designation: Senior Data Scientist (Lead Analytics Unit)
   - Department: Enterprise AI & Data Architecture
   - Date of Joining: March 15, 2022

2. STATEMENT OF FACTS & HOSTILE WORK ENVIRONMENT MISCONDUCT:
   Over the preceding six months (May 2024 to October 2024), Complainant was repeatedly subjected to persistent hostile work environment misconduct, inappropriate personal communications outside professional hours, and implied threats regarding quarterly bonus ratings by Senior Vice President of Analytics, Mr. Rajesh Verma.

   On August 22, 2024, Complainant was required to work past 10:30 PM for a critical client deployment. The company failed to provide verified night-shift transportation or security escort as required by statutory female employee safety protocols. Complainant was left unassisted at the office facility.

3. PROCEDURAL GRIEVANCE & INTERNAL HR FAILURE:
   - On September 5, 2024, Complainant submitted a formal written complaint detailing the misconduct to Head of Human Resources.
   - The company failed to forward the grievance to a statutory Internal Complaints Committee (ICC). In fact, no valid ICC headed by a senior woman officer had been constituted at the regional office facility.
   - No investigative interview was conducted, nor were interim protection measures implemented.

4. RETALIATORY ACTION & NOTICE OF TERMINATION:
   On October 10, 2024 (less than 35 days after filing the internal complaint), the company issued a Notice of Termination under the pretext of 'performance restructuring', despite Complainant having received 'Exceeds Expectations' performance ratings in the previous two annual reviews.

5. EVIDENTIARY ATTACHMENTS ON RECORD:
   - Incident Log #2024-0822 (Timestamped facility exit log at 10:42 PM; no cab assigned).
   - Email Communication chain dated Sept 5, 2024 (Formal HR grievance acknowledgment).
