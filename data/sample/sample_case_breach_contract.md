# Illustrative Case: Apex Logistics Solutions LLC v. Horizon Retail Enterprise Inc.
CASE_ID: CASE-2025-001
JURISDICTION: Delaware Commercial Court / Common Law
DATE_FILED: 2025-01-15

## Summary of Dispute
Horizon Retail Enterprise Inc. entered into a Master Logistics Service Agreement with Apex Logistics Solutions LLC on June 1, 2024. Horizon operates 45 retail stores and an online e-commerce platform. Apex contracted to provide dedicated fulfillment and refrigerated linehaul distribution.

Paragraph 14.2 of the Agreement stated:
'In the event Apex fails to deliver designated shipments within the guaranteed 48-hour delivery window, Apex shall pay liquidated damages of $5,000 per delayed truckload per day, regardless of actual loss.'

Between November 20 and November 28, 2024 (peak holiday shopping week), Apex suffered fleet driver shortages and routing software outages. Six critical linehaul shipments were delayed by 4 days. Horizon withheld payments totaling $120,000 claiming liquidated damages under Paragraph 14.2. Horizon also claimed an additional $350,000 in lost customer goodwill and lost sales.

Apex contends that:
1. The liquidated damages clause constitutes an unenforceable penalty because actual freight cost was only $1,200 per truckload, making $5,000/day disproportionate.
2. The delay was caused by an unprecedented third-party cloud outage affecting telematics (arguing force majeure / commercial impracticability).
3. The $350,000 consequential damages claim violates the rule of Hadley v. Baxendale as peak customer goodwill loss was never in contemplation of the carrier.
