Statute Title: Workplace Safety & Sexual Harassment Protection Act (POSH & Common Law Duty of Care)
Source Type: statute
Jurisdiction: Common Law / Statutory Protection
Citation: Statutory Code § 354A / Workplace Safety Act § 4-11

SECTION 4: MANDATORY CONSTITUTION OF INTERNAL COMPLAINTS COMMITTEE (ICC)
Every employer employing ten or more workers is statutorily mandated to constitute an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) at every office or administrative unit. The Committee must be headed by a senior woman employee and include independent third-party members. Failure to constitute an ICC renders any workplace disciplinary action or termination procedurally void and subjects the employer to statutory financial penalties and operational suspension.

SECTION 7: DUTY OF CARE & NIGHT SHIFT TRANSPORTATION PROTOCOLS
Employers engaging female employees during evening or night shifts (between 7:00 PM and 6:00 AM) must provide dedicated, employer-managed secure transportation, verified driver credentials, GPS tracking, and security escorts. Failure to maintain verified night transportation protocols constitutes a strict liability breach of statutory workplace safety duty of care.

SECTION 11: PROHIBITION OF RETALIATORY DISCHARGE & VICTIMIZATION
No employer shall take adverse employment action, issue retaliatory termination notices, alter employment terms, or create a hostile environment against an employee for filing a safety complaint or participating in an ICC inquiry. Any termination executed within 180 days of an internal complaint raises a rebuttable presumption of unlawful retaliatory discharge.
